## 7. AI UI specification (atom level)
Tokens and components come from `docs/ui-spec.md`, `docs/ui-spec-app.md` and `docs/ui-spec-editor-v2.md` (Button, Input, Select, Badge, Dialog, Menu, Popover, Toast, InfoBox, segmented control, switch row, progress bar, ListRow, SettingsSection, ConfirmDialog, EmptyState, skeletons, image field, page switcher, Page tips). Reuse; do not duplicate.

### 7.1 Shared AI components
- **`CreditChip`:** height 22, padding 0 8, radius 999, 12/700, bg `--accent-soft`, text `--accent-text`, coins icon 12 and text "About 3 credits" (compact: "3"). When the balance is too low: bg `--danger-soft`, text `--danger-text`, "Needs 3 credits". Tooltip (and `aria-describedby`): "You have {n} credits left." Always rendered next to AI buttons that cost credits; hidden when the cost would be zero.
- **`AiButton`:** variants: `secondary` sm (height 36, padding 0 12, 14/600, sparkles icon 16 in `--accent-text`, label "Write with AI" or the specific label), `ghost-icon` (36x36 sparkles icon button with aria-label), `primary` md and lg for main actions ("Create my website with AI"). States: default, hover, loading (spinner 16 and label "Writing..."), disabled-by-kill-switch (opacity 0.5, tooltip "AI is paused for a short while."), out-of-credits (looks normal, opens the out-of-credits dialog on click). Clicking never starts an action costing 10 credits or more without the confirm dialog (7.3); smaller actions start immediately.
- **`AiResultCard`:** bg `--surface-muted`, radius 12, padding 12, text 15/1.5 (max-height 200 scroll, `white-space: pre-wrap`); header row (label 12/700 caps "AI suggestion", `CreditChip` showing what was **charged**); body; flagged items InfoBox (warning variant, per item: excerpt in `mark` style (accent soft background), reason, buttons "Keep" and "Remove" ghost sm); footer buttons "Use this" (primary sm), "Try again" (secondary sm with `CreditChip`), "Cancel" (ghost sm), thumbs up and down 36x36 ghost icon buttons with aria-labels "This was good" and "This was not good" (down opens a Menu of reasons), and a 12 muted line "AI can make mistakes. Check the facts."
- **`AiProgress`:** vertical list of steps (28 px circles per the domain Stepper style: done check, current spinner, waiting ring), each with label 15/600 and time 12 muted; below a bar showing step count and "Usually {n} to {m} seconds". Cancel ghost sm with a ConfirmDialog when a cost has been incurred ("Stop now? You will pay for the work already done.").
- **`SampleChip`** (from templates) reused for AI placeholders ("Sample").
- **Low-credits banner:** InfoBox accent (padding 12 16, radius 12, coins icon 18) shown at the top of the editor panel and the AI credits page when balance is under 20 percent of the last grant or under 5: "You have {n} AI credits left." with "See plans" ghost sm (Free) or "How credits work" link.
- **Out-of-credits dialog** (width 440, sheet on mobile): coins icon in a 48 circle (`--accent-soft`), H3 "You are out of AI credits", text 15 muted: Free: "Your free credits are used up. Upgrade for monthly credits, or keep writing by hand." Paid: "Your credits refill on {date}." Buttons: "See plans" (primary md, Free and Basic only), "How credits work" (secondary md), "Close" (ghost md). A row "Earn credits" with "Invite friends" disabled and a "Coming soon" pill until the referral programme exists (the component checks a feature flag).
- **Confirm dialog for big actions** (width 480, sheet on mobile): sparkles tile 48, H3 e.g. "Write your whole website?", a summary list (what will be created), then an info grid: "About {n} credits" (Bricolage 700 28), "You have {m} credits", "After this: {m-n}" (warning colour when under 5). Muted line "You are only charged for what AI actually uses. If something goes wrong, you are not charged." Buttons "Cancel" and the primary action. A checkbox "Do not ask again for actions under 20 credits" (stored per user).

### 7.3 Wizard: "Create it for me with AI"

If the user has too few credits: InfoBox warning "You need about {m} credits and have {n}." with "Use the standard design instead" (selects that card) and "See plans". If AI is paused: the card is disabled with the pause tooltip.
- **New step "Tell us more"** (inserted after step 4 only when AI is chosen; total steps 7): H1 28 "Tell us about your business", sub 16 muted "The more you tell us, the better your words. Only share what is true." Fields (gap 20, each with label 14/600 and helper 14 muted): "What do you offer?" (chips input: type and press Enter or comma; each chip height 36, padding 0 12, radius 999, bg `--surface-muted`, with x button 20; max 8 items, 40 characters each; helper "For example: Haircuts, Hair colour, Beard trims"); "Who are your customers?" Textarea (min 96, max 200, counter, placeholder "For example: Busy people who want a quick, friendly haircut."); "What makes you different? (optional)" Textarea (max 200; helper "Only things that are true. For example: Open late on Fridays."); "Where are you?" Input (max 80, placeholder "Kigali, Nyarugenge" ... generic "City or area"); "How should it sound?" tone chips (multi-select up to 2: Friendly, Professional, Bold, Calm, Playful, Elegant; height 40, selected state like other chips); "Anything we should avoid? (optional)" Input (max 100); "Which pages do you want?" checklist (rows 48 high, checkbox 24): Home (locked on), About, Services (labelled by business type: Menu, Products, Projects, Services), Contact, Questions (FAQ), Gallery; limited by the plan's page allowance: Free shows a single note "Your Free plan has one page. We will put everything on it." and hides the checklist; Basic limit 5; show "{n} of {limit}" counter; "Create a hero picture too" switch (only when images are enabled; helper "Costs about {n} more credits."; default off). A language note InfoBox (info) appears when the website language is not English: "AI works best in English. Please check the result carefully."
- The fields are saved as drafts. Continue is enabled when "What do you offer?" has at least one chip or the description from step 3 is at least 20 characters.
- **Step "Check and confirm" (summary)** adds a row "AI" with "AI will write your website. About {n} credits." and the primary button label "Create my website with AI" (loading "Starting...").
- **Generation page** (route `/dashboard/projects/{id}/ai-setup?request={id}`, full-screen, no app shell, top bar 64 with logo mark and "Save and exit" disabled until a safe point): centered Card (max-width 560, padding 32, radius 24): H1 24 "Writing your website", text 15 muted "This usually takes {range}. You can wait here or come back later.", `AiProgress` with six steps: "Understanding your business", "Choosing a design", "Planning your pages", "Writing your words" (sub-lines list page names as each completes: "Home page written", with check icons), "Checking everything", "Building your website"; credits shown "Credits used so far: {n}" updating; a ghost "Cancel" button. On leaving and returning, the page resumes from the operation. On failure: card swaps to danger layout (56 circle `--danger-soft`, H3 "We could not write your website", text from the error map, "You were not charged.", buttons "Try again" (primary md; creates a new request), "Use the standard design" (secondary md; goes to the project Overview), "Contact support" (ghost md)). On refusal: H3 "We cannot write that", text `AI_REFUSED`, button "Change my answers" (returns to the questions step).
- **Success view:** 72 circle `--success-soft` with sparkles icon 32 `--success`, H2 "Your website is written", text 16 muted "Take a look and change anything you like. Nothing is live until you publish."; a summary card (rows: Pages created ("3 pages"), Design (palette name with five 20 px dots and the font names), Credits used ("7")); **"Things to check"** card (InfoBox warning) when review items exist: "{n} things to check before you publish" with the first three items listed and "See all" link to the Tips panel; buttons "Review my website" (primary lg; opens the editor homepage with `?setup=1`), "Preview" (secondary md), and an "Undo and use the standard design" ghost md (restores the restore point with a ConfirmDialog). A feedback row (thumbs up and down).
- The wizard saves AI answers into `project_settings.business_facts` (offerings as facts, location) and `brand_voice` (tones, audience, avoid) so later AI actions stay consistent; the user sees this in "Writing style".

### 7.5 Editor: field level AI
- **Field labels:** every text Input and Textarea in Edit panels that maps to a catalogue `textProp` gets a 32x32 ghost icon button (sparkles 16 in `--accent-text`) at the right of its label row (aria-label "Write {field name} with AI"). Disabled and hidden for viewers.
- **AI popover** (width 340; bottom sheet on mobile; radius 16; padding 12; shadow raised; z-index 60; focus trapped; Escape closes): header row (14/700 "Write with AI", right: balance chip "{n} credits left" neutral 12/700); **actions list** (rows min-height 44, padding 0 12, radius 10, icon 18 `--accent-text`, label 15/500, `CreditChip` compact at the right; hover bg `--row-hover`): when the field is empty or very short (under 20 characters): "Write it for me"; otherwise: "Make it shorter", "Make it longer", "Fix spelling and grammar", "Make it friendlier", "Make it more professional", "Use simpler words", "Write it again differently"; always last: an Input "Or tell AI what to do" (height 44, placeholder "For example: mention free delivery", max 200, counter) with a "Go" primary sm button. Selecting runs the action (the popover shows `AiProgress` compact: spinner and "Writing..."), then the **`AiResultCard`**. "Use this" applies the text to the field and canvas as **one undo step** and closes; flagged claims block "Use this" until each is kept or removed. "Try again" re-runs with the same action. Errors appear inline (danger InfoBox with the mapped message and "Try again").
- **Rich text (Text block):** the floating format toolbar gets a sparkles button after the list button (36x36, white icon on the dark toolbar): applies the same actions to the **selected text only** (or the whole block when nothing is selected) through the same popover anchored to the toolbar; applying replaces the selection and keeps surrounding formatting.
- **Block level:** the floating block toolbar gets a sparkles icon button (40x40, tooltip "Improve with AI") after Duplicate; it opens the right panel's **AI** tab for that block (a fourth tab "AI" next to Edit, Style, Section for blocks that have text): content: segmented control "Improve all text", "Rewrite for a purpose", "Add items" (only for list blocks); **Improve all text:** the same action list as above applied to every text field of the block at once (one call), result shown as a list of fields with before and after (each row: field name 13/600, before in muted strikethrough-free text, after in `--text`; per-row checkbox, all checked), "Use selected" primary md and "Try again". **Rewrite for a purpose:** Select "Purpose" (headline that sells, friendly welcome, explain a service, build trust, invite to act, short and simple) then the result flow. **Add items:** stepper control "How many" (1 to 6, default 3), Input "What kind of items?" (optional), run `list.expand`; results appear as item cards (each with a checkbox, price fields empty and an InfoBox "Add your own prices"), "Add selected" primary md.
- Keyboard shortcut `Ctrl or Cmd + J` opens the AI popover for the focused field; listed in the shortcuts dialog.
- **Undo:** all AI applies produce one editor undo entry labelled "AI: {action}" (shown in the History panel as an auto version entry too when a page-level change happens).
